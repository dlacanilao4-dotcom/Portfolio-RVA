ARTIST STATEMENT

This portfolio shows my work from different modules where I learned the basics of art. Each task helped me practice new skills like drawing, taking photos, and making digital designs. I learned that I can share my ideas through pictures instead of just using words. Some of my projects look like real life, while others are more creative and abstract. I also included Filipino-inspired designs to show my culture in a simple way.


In the "Process Documentation" section, you can see how I worked on my ideas step by step. It shows my journey from simple sketches and rough drafts to the finished artwork. This portfolio represents my progress as a beginner. Even though I am still learning, every module helped me become more creative and patient. I am proud to show how much I have improved my style and techniques this semester.
